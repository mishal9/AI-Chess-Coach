Score 60 pieces of chess coaching

Each item is coaching written about one real game that a chess engine has already analysed. You get the game on a board and the engine's findings next to the coaching. Moves the engine flagged are marked in amber, and the board opens on the first of them. Treat the findings as ground truth and judge the coaching written from them. You don't need to be a strong player.

About 2–3 minutes per item. Your scores save as you go, so you can stop and come back.

Four scores per item, 1 to 5
Ground rules
  • Items are in random order, and several different systems wrote them. Don't try to work out which; score what's in front of you.
  • Some games appear more than once with different coaching. Score each item on its own.
  • If you're torn between two scores, take the lower one.
  • A comment is optional, but helpful when something is plainly wrong.
